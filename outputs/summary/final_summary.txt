Group 14 Project Cycle 2 Final Summary

Dataset:
YRBS_2007.csv

Research Questions:
1. Is the proportion of students who felt sad or hopeless different from 0.30?
2. Is the mean height of students different from 1.70 meters?

Selected Variables:
- Proportion analysis: SadOrHopeless
- Mean analysis: HowTallAreYouWithoutShoesInMeters

EDA Highlights:
- The proportion of students who felt sad or hopeless was close to 0.30.
- The height distribution was fairly concentrated, with only a small number of outliers.

Inference Results:
- Sample proportion = 0.3000
- 95% CI for population proportion = (0.2924, 0.3077)
- One-sample proportion test p-value = 0.992601
- Decision: Fail to reject H0

- Sample mean height = 1.6940 meters
- 95% CI for population mean = (1.6923, 1.6958)
- One-sample t-test p-value < 0.001
- Decision: Reject H0

Final Conclusion:
The proportion of students who felt sad or hopeless was not significantly different from 0.30. However, the mean height of students was significantly different from 1.70 meters.
